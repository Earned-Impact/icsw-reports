Meta Ads Are Driving JPC Traffic

The connection between Meta advertising and JPC website traffic is unmistakable this month. Paid Social is ICSW’s second-largest traffic channel at 1,783 sessions (31% of all traffic), and the JPC Neuroscience & Psychodynamic Psychotherapy page is the #1 landing page on the entire site at 1,797 sessions (32% of all traffic). Those numbers nearly mirror each other — Meta ads are sending visitors directly to JPC, and they’re converting.
