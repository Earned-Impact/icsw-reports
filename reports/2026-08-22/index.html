Website

Website Development Update

All updates from the feedback we received from the ICSW team mid-week have been completed, concluding the QC process. We have also successfully accessed the domain through EDUCAUSE.

The final step before we can begin the site migration is a decision on hosting. Because the current site is hosted on Squarespace and the new site is built on WordPress, a different hosting provider will be needed. There are two options:

Option A: Transfer the domain to our hosting account. This is the simpler, lower-risk path because we manage the migration end to end. Pricing ranges from $25 to $50 per month depending on whether we host on a shared server or a dedicated one.
Option B: Move the new site to a hosting provider of ICSW's choice. Because Squarespace cannot host a WordPress site, this would mean selecting a new provider on your end. This option requires more coordination but is doable.

Once we have a decision on hosting, we can finalize and begin the migration of the site for launch.

Email

Email Campaigns

We sent email campaigns on August 20 to three ICSW audiences promoting the Latinx and JPC continuing education programs. We ran A/B subject line tests on the PhD and General audiences.

  • PhD audience (916 recipients): "Keep Growing Your Clinical Skills This Fall" won the A/B test with an 11.1% open rate.
  • MA audience (502 recipients): 9.7% open rate, 0.4% click rate.
  • ICSW General (3,792 recipients): "Explore Virtual Continuing Education at ICSW This Fall" won the A/B test with a 16.9% open rate and 43 unique clicks. We also tested send times for this group.
