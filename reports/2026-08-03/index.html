July finished strong with 35 total conversions across Search and PMax, up from 27 at the July 27 mark. The Certificate Programs search campaign ended the month as the top performer by volume with 722 clicks and a 16.32% CTR. Its top ad ("Earn CEUs at ICSW") delivered a 25.42% CTR across 2,557 impressions, making it the most-clicked ad in the account.

PMax continued to be the primary conversion driver with 21 of 35 total conversions at a cost per conversion of about $24. PMax spend was down nearly 30% versus June while still increasing conversions, showing strong efficiency gains.

The two "virtual certificate" keyword variants combined for 631 clicks at 31–32% CTR, confirming strong demand for online certificate offerings. PhD and Masters search campaigns both grew spend versus June as those programs ramp into the fall enrollment push.

Google Ads campaigns drove over 1,100 sessions to the website in July, with strong on-site engagement. Visitors from paid ads spent an average of 40 seconds on site with a 45.4% engagement rate and over 5 events per session—meaning nearly half of all ad-driven visitors actively explored program pages. The Certificate Programs search campaign was the top contributor, generating the most ad clicks of any campaign in the account.

Organic search continued to lead overall traffic with over 4,000 sessions and a 55.5% engagement rate, confirming strong baseline demand for ICSW’s programs.

The PhD list showed the strongest engagement with a 16.0% open rate on the first email—well above the MA lists (8.6–11.9%). The PhD list also had the highest click-through on Email 1 at 1.4% clicks per unique open. This suggests the PhD audience is more actively engaged and closer to decision stage.

Open rates on Email 2 (August 3) ranged from 8.5% to 10.2% across all three lists. Click rates are low so far, which is expected since Email 2 just went out and these are still early reads. Resends to non-openers and the final email on August 5 will push cumulative reach higher.

Bounce rates remained healthy across all sends (0.3–1.4%) and unsubscribes stayed under 1%, indicating good list quality.

New Listserv: Fall Programs Email

A new email campaign is being launched to a broader listserv promoting ICSW's full slate of fall programs. The email highlights three continuing education certificates—Integrative Psychoanalytic Couple Therapy, Latinx-Centered Psychodynamic Consultation Group, and the Palombo Center Neuroscience & Psychodynamic Psychotherapy Certificate—alongside the PhD and MA degree programs (application deadline August 10). This is designed to reach new audiences beyond the existing prospect lists and drive fall enrollment across all program types. Full email copy is in the draft document.
