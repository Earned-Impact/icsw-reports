Across all of October, EI's paid campaigns have turned ICSW's continuing education content into the highest-traffic section of the website. The JPC Neuroscience & Psychodynamic Psychotherapy page alone accounts for 28.3% of all site sessions, making it the single most visited page on icsw.edu.

Ad Group Trends

Compared to the prior period (September 3 to 30), the Certificate Programs ad group held steady with only a 2.3% decrease in spend. The Neuroscience & Psychodynamic ad group grew 11.7%, and the Latinx-Centered Psychotherapy group increased 125% (from a small base, reflecting early traction). Generic Continuing Education spend decreased 35.8%, with budget shifting toward the more targeted program-specific groups.
