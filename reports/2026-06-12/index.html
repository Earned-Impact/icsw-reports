Certificate Programs: Fall 2026 Strategy

With three certificate programs launching alongside MA and PhD this fall, we have put together a coordinated marketing strategy to give each program visibility while keeping the budget focused and efficient. Below is a summary of the approach — the full strategy and timeline is linked at the bottom for detailed review ahead of our next call.

The Three Programs

JPC Certificate
Dir: Bill
For post-master's clinicians with 5+ years experience seeking advanced clinical formation. Program page and "at a glance" overview are complete — this will serve as the template for all certificate pages.
Latinx Consultation Group
Dir: Mirari
Phase 2 of the existing program. Prior cohort members can re-enroll, providing a built-in base. New program page to be built matching the JPC template.
Couples Therapy Certificate
Dir: Carla
Established program with strong name recognition through Carla's reputation. Page content exists but will be reorganized into the unified template format.

Marketing Approach

  • Unified certificate landing page. Rather than running separate ad campaigns for each program, we will drive traffic to a single certificates page where visitors can explore all three options. This keeps ad spend concentrated, avoids keyword cannibalization across five programs, and lets visitors discover programs they may not have been searching for.
  • Program directors as organic recruiters. Bill and Carla in particular have strong personal brands that will drive organic interest. We will draft outreach content (emails, LinkedIn posts) for directors to send through their own channels and listservs — a message from them carries more weight than one from the institution alone.
  • Phased budget allocation. MA and PhD remain the primary ad spend focus through their deadlines. As MA wraps, that budget shifts toward certificate promotion for the final summer push. Certificates have flexible enrollment through the start of term, giving us more runway.
  • Page uniformity. All certificate pages will follow Bill's JPC template for a consistent, reader-friendly experience. This was flagged as important for brand professionalism.

What We Need from Program Directors

Bill's "at a glance" overview has been a great model. To build targeted marketing for each program, we need the same from Mirari and Carla:

  • Who should enroll? Target audience and prerequisites
  • What makes it unique? Key differentiators and outcomes
  • Program logistics: Duration, format, start date

This gives us what we need for ad targeting, keyword strategy, and email campaign messaging — without guesswork.
