The Certificate Programs search campaign continues to outperform, posting a 15.54% CTR driven almost entirely by the "virtual certificate programs" keyword (31.28% CTR, 280 clicks). This is a new campaign that launched earlier this month and has quickly become the most efficient search campaign in the account.

PMax remains the primary conversion driver with 18 of 27 total conversions this month, at a cost per conversion of roughly $24. PMax spend is down 32% compared to June, while conversions have nearly doubled from the July 20 mark (10 to 18), a sign the campaign is becoming more efficient as it learns.

PhD search ad groups saw significant growth this period, with the "Clinical Social Work" and "Social Work Doctoral" groups up 136% and 112% respectively in spend versus June.
