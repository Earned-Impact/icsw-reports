JPC Program Q&A Session

Date: Tuesday, October 6, 2026
Time: 2:30 - 3:30 PM CT
Host: William "Bill" Gieseke, Ph.D.
Where: Zoom (free, registration required)

The JPC is a certificate program that integrates neuroscience with psychotherapy practice, designed for clinicians who want to deepen their understanding of how the brain informs therapeutic work.

The session will be led by Dr. Bill Gieseke, ICSW Core Faculty member, Executive Director of the Palombo Center, and faculty at the Chicago Psychoanalytic Institute. Bill is a founding member of the Palombo Center Advisory Committee and has led many of its conferences. He brings deep expertise in psychoanalytic approaches and a firsthand understanding of the ICSW community, having earned his own Ph.D. from ICSW.

Whether you're exploring the program for the first time or ready to apply, this is your chance to hear directly from Bill about what the program covers, who it's designed for, and what participants walk away with.

In this session, you'll have a chance to:

  • Learn about the JPC curriculum and what makes it unique
  • Hear from Bill about his vision for the program and what participants gain
  • Ask questions about schedule, commitment, and enrollment
  • Get a feel for whether the program is the right fit for you

Space is open, but registration is required so we can send you the Zoom link.
