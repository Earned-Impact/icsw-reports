As part of our commitment to supporting the ongoing professional development of the ICSW community, we're excited to share an upcoming continuing education opportunity focused on couples therapy.

Striving for Excellence in Couples Therapy: Zeroing in on What Matters

Date: Friday, October 2, 2026
Time: 11:00 AM - 12:30 PM CT
Presenter: Enrico Gnaulati, PhD
Credits: 1.5 CEs

Dr. Gnaulati is a clinical psychologist with over 30 years of experience in private practice and an Affiliate Professor at Seattle University. He is the author of four books, including Flourishing Love: A Secular Guide to Lasting Intimate Relationships, and has published widely on couples therapy in both academic journals and outlets like The Atlantic and Salon.

In this 90-minute session, Dr. Gnaulati will explore what distinguishes effective couples therapy from sessions that stall - helping clinicians zero in on the interventions and moments that drive real progress. Whether you're building a couples practice or looking to sharpen your current approach, this is a chance to earn 1.5 CE credits while learning from one of the field's most experienced voices.
