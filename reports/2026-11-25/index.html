Where Are Ads Showing?

84.5% of clicks are coming through Google Search Partners. These are other search engines and websites that partner with Google to show ads (such as YouTube search results, Google Maps, and smaller search engines). The remaining 15.5% come from Google Search directly. This is normal and means the ads are reaching people searching for programs across a wider range of places beyond just google.com. Mobile devices account for 88.8% of all ad clicks.
