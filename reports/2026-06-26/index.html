What Comes Next

  • We are currently in the content import phase. Pages are built and styled, and legacy content (dissertations, forms, faculty profiles) is being loaded in. Once content is populated and pages are finalized, the next step is internal linking and navigation.
  • Interlinking comes last by design. Wiring up navigation and cross-page links before the page structure is locked usually means redoing it when pages move or change names. We will start interlinking once no major pages are being added or restructured.
  • Nothing changes on the live site until everything has been reviewed and approved. All work continues on the staging environment.
