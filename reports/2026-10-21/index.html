Enrollment CRM & Student Journey

Damon joined today’s call to discuss the enrollment visibility tool. Key context: ICSW is locked into Blackbaud for approximately 4 more years due to an auto-renewal clause in the prior administration’s contract. A full CRM / student information system replacement is being budgeted for years 3–4 of that contract.

In the meantime, Damon is scoping a cost-effective “bandaid” solution that would give Jesse visibility into the full student journey: website visit → inquiry form → application → admissions → enrollment. The system would run on email notifications (BCC to a shared inbox), provide a dashboard with green/yellow/red status indicators, and flag anything that falls outside response-time tolerances.

Damon also raised SMS as a future channel (Twilio-based, very low cost at ICSW’s scale) and is researching a nonprofit carve-out for peer-to-peer texting that could support outreach campaigns.

Timeline

Damon will scope capabilities, constraints, and cost over the next ~2 weeks and reconvene with Jesse. Target implementation: mid-January 2027.

Jesse to provide Damon with a visual map of the student journey (stages, on-ramps, off-ramps, and where phone calls / emails happen). This is the #1 input Damon needs to begin scoping.
