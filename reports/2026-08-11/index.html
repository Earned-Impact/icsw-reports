Website

The new ICSW website is in the final stages of quality assurance. As part of migrating content from the existing site to the new platform, our team completed a full content and responsiveness review to ensure everything transferred correctly.

QC Summary

  • 400 to 500 content corrections were identified and fixed during the migration. These were issues inherited from the original site, including incorrect number formatting, extra spacing, and spelling errors that had been present in the existing content.
  • Over 45% of buttons needed updated links as part of the migration to the new site structure. All have been corrected, with a second pass planned after the domain switch to confirm nothing was affected.
  • Placeholder text on several pages where original content had not yet been migrated has been located and replaced.
  • A small number of responsiveness issues remain and are being addressed by the development team. Once resolved, the site is ready for launch.

Content Items Requiring ICSW Review

During QC, several content accuracy questions came up that we need your help confirming before launch:

  • Master's tuition (Part Time card): The per-credit rate is listed as $1,014.81, and the Curriculum Overview states 60 credit hours, which would total $60,888.60. However, the total shown on the page is $76,884.46. Can you confirm the correct per-credit rate, credit count, and total?
  • Part-time credit cap: The Part Time card states "Max. 9 credits / sem" over 3 years. At 9 credits per semester across 3 years, that caps at 54 credits, but the program requires 60. Should the per-semester cap or the duration be adjusted?
  • Academic year dates: The hero section and Cost of Attendance page still reference the "2025 to 2026 Academic Year." Should these be updated to 2026 to 2027, or labeled as the current published rate?
  • Licensure documents: Two of the eight licensure document cards (under "ICSW State Disclosures for Licensure" and "Illinois Examination Supplement") do not have working links. Can you provide the PDFs?
  • Exam availability note: The Illinois State Licensure Examinations section references exams being "now available throughout the entire month (updated November 7, 2022)." Can you confirm this is still accurate, or should the date be updated?
  • Student Handbook: The Documents and Downloads section currently offers the 2025 to 2026 Student Handbook. Should the 2026 to 2027 version be uploaded?
  • Berzoff Scholarship deadline: The Dr. Joan Berzoff Social Services Scholarship page does not list an application deadline anywhere. Can you provide the deadline so we can add it?

The staging site is available for review at icsw.tryearnedimpact.com.
