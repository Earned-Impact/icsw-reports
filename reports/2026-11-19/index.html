Where Conversions Are Coming From

The top converting search term is "clinical social work phd programs," which has driven 6 of the 20 total conversions from 132 clicks. Of the 20 total conversions, approximately 8 are PhD-specific. The remaining conversions are spread across broader counseling and graduate program terms in the Masters campaign.

PhD-specific terms are driving the highest conversion volume. Broader counseling terms like "online degree to become counselor" are converting at a notably high click-through rate (65.12%), capturing prospective Masters students who see ICSW's information and click through at a strong rate.

The majority of clicks (87.1%) are coming through Google Search Partners, with 12.9% from Google Search directly. Mobile devices account for 90.2% of all ad clicks.

Faculty listings are now in alphabetical order. Randy implemented this change and it is live on the site.

Per feedback from the Provost and student input, dissertations will be grouped by year (most recent first), with alphabetical ordering within each year group. The team is exploring the best way to implement this, potentially using expandable year sections that display alphabetically when opened.

The "Academic Documents" tab will be renamed to "Handbooks and Catalogs" per Denise's request. Documents will also be reorganized so they are separated by section, allowing students who are unsure which tab to click to still locate what they need. Fida is picking this up from Randy's initial work.

Denise reported that the course schedule images appear very small on her MacBook Air, though they display correctly on mobile and other devices. The team was unable to replicate the issue on other machines but Fida is investigating what could be triggering the display problem on her specific setup.
