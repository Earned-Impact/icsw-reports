Month-to-Date Highlights

  • The Certificate Programs search campaign launched this week and is already the highest-CTR campaign at 10.16%. Early top performers include "virtual certificate programs" (11 clicks, 39.29% CTR) and the Certificate Programs ad group (12 clicks, 30.77% CTR). Strong signal that this audience is highly responsive to search ads.
  • PMax is the clear conversion leader, driving 10 of 13 total conversions at $1.17 average CPC. It also generated the most clicks (248) across all campaigns.
  • PhD continues to outperform Masters on efficiency. PhD drove 45 clicks at an 8.67% CTR versus Masters at 39 clicks and 4.39% CTR. Masters has a higher spend ($289 vs $185), so PhD is delivering significantly more traffic per dollar.
  • "Clinical social work phd programs" remains the most efficient keyword at 11.21% CTR with 25 clicks. "Mental health counseling education" is the highest-volume keyword (26 clicks) but at a lower 4.85% CTR and higher cost ($194.72).
  • Search CPC has risen slightly to $4.89, up from $4.71 at the mid-month mark. Still well below the June average of $7.82.

This fall, ICSW is offering three professional development programs for clinicians who want to sharpen their clinical skills, earn continuing education credits, and learn from nationally recognized faculty, all in a virtual format designed for working professionals.

Integrative Psychoanalytic Couple Therapy Certificate

9 months · Virtual · Thursdays · Starts September 2026

Now recruiting for its 6th cohort, this program has trained over 55 clinicians from 5 countries in psychoanalytic couple therapy. Led by Dr. Carla Leone, Dr. Arthur Nielsen (Harvard/Johns Hopkins, 40+ publications, 2 textbooks), and Dr. Karen Bloomberg, the program combines didactic learning, small-group case conferences, and individual consultation. Scholarship assistance is available, with priority given to BIPOC applicants and those serving marginalized populations.

$3,600 tuition  |  Space limited to 12 participants

Latinx-Centered Psychodynamic Consultation Group

Dean's Distinguished Speaker Series

9 sessions · Virtual Wednesdays 3–5 PM CST · September 2026 to May 2027

A monthly consultation group designed specifically for Latinx therapists and mental health professionals seeking culturally grounded psychodynamic peer consultation. Participants discuss live clinical material, engage with contemporary psychodynamic literature, and build lasting professional relationships. Co-led by Dr. Merari Fernandez, PhD, LCSW, and Dr. Kelly G. Arteaga, PsyD. Returning participants from prior cohorts are welcome to re-enroll.

$440  |  18 CE credits

Joseph Palombo Center: Neuroscience & Psychodynamic Psychotherapy Certificate

24 weeks · Virtual Fridays 1–4 PM CT · August 2026 to May 2027

An advanced postgraduate certificate for clinicians who want neuroscience to deepen (not replace) psychodynamic understanding. Grounded in Joseph Palombo's model of the self as a complex adaptive system, the program integrates neuroscience, attachment, affect regulation, trauma, and psychodynamic formulation into a rigorous clinical sequence. Led by William D. Gieseke, Ph.D.

$3,100  |  72 CEU hours  |  Application deadline: July 30, 2026
