Enrollment Tracking

Conversion Tracking & Enrollment Dashboard

A key discussion this week centered on closing the gap between ad clicks and actual enrollment. Currently, we can see that someone clicked "Request Info" on the website, but we cannot confirm whether they actually submitted the form.

New Tracking Capability

The EI team identified a solution: by placing a tracking code on the thank you confirmation page that appears after a form submission, we can track completed submissions, not just clicks. Combined with UTM parameters on each program page (MA, PhD, CE), this will allow us to follow the full funnel from ad click to form submission for the first time.

Kaelene is connecting with Randy to explore what is possible here.

Enrollment Dashboard

Jesse raised the enrollment dashboard that Damon previously built out. With ICSW now in this year's budget, the conversation is shifting toward realistic implementation. Jesse noted that tracking the full inquiry journey (from ad to site visit to form submission to enrollment) is a key KPI in ICSW's strategic plan.

Next week's call will be dedicated to mapping out the full tracking pipeline and putting the remaining pieces together.

ICSW generated 455 inquiries and 55 submitted applications this cycle. Jesse flagged that the source attribution for these inquiries is incomplete. The new tracking capabilities and enrollment dashboard will help close that gap and give EI proper credit for ad-driven leads.
