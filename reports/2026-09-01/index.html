Behind-the-Scenes Work This Month

As part of the new website build, several of the page URLs on the ICSW site changed. Because the Google Ads campaigns were still pointing to the old URLs, this caused a few issues that our team identified and resolved during August:

  • Broken ad links fixed: With the new website in place, some of the links in your Google Ads—including key pages like admissions and schedules—were leading to "page not found" errors (404s) because those URLs had changed. Our team updated all links across the ads to point to the correct new pages. This also uncovered a tracking issue: because the ads had been pointing to the main website instead of the dedicated landing page, conversions were not being recorded properly. We corrected the tracking setup so all future form submissions and inquiries are captured accurately.
  • Broken sitelinks paused: We audited the extra links that appear below your main ad (called "sitelinks") and paused any that were leading to error pages due to the URL changes, so every link a prospective student clicks takes them to a working, relevant page.
  • Ads temporarily paused for website DNS updates: While we made updates to the website domain settings (DNS) as part of the site migration, we proactively paused the ads for a short period to make sure no visitors encountered errors during the transition. Ads were restarted once the changes took effect.
  • Google account verification resolved: Mid-month, Google paused the ads account because a verification deadline had passed. Our team worked to complete the verification process and successfully reactivated the account. To prevent this from happening again, we are now setting up a direct relationship with a Google account manager who will help us stay on top of account health and proactively flag any issues before they impact your campaigns.
Resolved

All of the above issues have been fully resolved. Your Certificate Programs campaign is live and running smoothly, and we have stronger safeguards in place going forward.

What This Means

Accessibility compliance is both a best practice and, for many institutions, a legal requirement. Having an accessible website ensures all prospective students—including those using screen readers or other assistive technology—can navigate and engage with your content.

Proposed Update

The JPC card remains unchanged since that program is still accepting applications. For the filled programs, here is what happens when a visitor clicks "Stay Informed":

The Visitor Experience

Step 1: Visitor clicks "Stay Informed"

They are taken to the full program page where they can read about the program, the instructors, the format, and what they would gain from it. This builds interest and keeps them engaged rather than turning them away.

Step 2: They see a short sign-up form

At the top of the program page, there is a note that the fall 2026 cohort is at capacity, along with a simple form (name and email) to be the first to know when the next cohort opens.

Step 3: Their information enters our nurture pipeline

Once they submit the form, they are added to a segmented email list for that specific program. From there, we can send them updates when the next cohort opens, as well as other relevant opportunities such as fall presentations, the Dean's Distinguished Series, and other continuing education offerings throughout the year.

Step 4: Ongoing nurture keeps them engaged

Rather than losing these prospective students, we keep them warm with periodic updates. When the next enrollment period opens, they are already familiar with ICSW and ready to act. This same list also serves as a foundation for a broader continuing education newsletter if ICSW chooses to go that route in the future.

This approach turns a "closed" program into a lead generation tool. Every visitor who clicks "Stay Informed" is someone who has already shown interest. By capturing their information now, we build a qualified database that grows over time and directly supports future enrollment cycles.

4 hours per month are allocated for SEO services (keyword tracking, on-page optimizations, metadata, and technical adjustments). An additional 2 hours per month cover website content edits, page adjustments, and troubleshooting. Website support beyond those 2 hours is available at $35/hour.

Flex Hours (8 hours/month)

8 hours per month of flex time are available for ad-hoc needs, which can include website updates when needed. These hours are reconciled quarterly to accommodate busy months.

In practice, this means routine content updates, page edits, and smaller design adjustments can be handled within the existing monthly hours. For larger changes—like adding new pages or significant redesigns—we would scope those together and determine whether they fit within flex time or should be scoped separately.
